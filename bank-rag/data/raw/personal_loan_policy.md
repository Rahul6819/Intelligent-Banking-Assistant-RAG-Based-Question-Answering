# Sample Bank Personal Loan Policy (SYNTHETIC - for demo only)

## 1. Eligibility
Salaried applicants aged 21 to 60 years with a minimum monthly income of Rs 25,000 are eligible for a personal loan. Self-employed applicants need at least 2 years of business vintage and an annual ITR showing income of Rs 4 lakh or more. A credit score of 700 or above is required for approval.

## 2. Loan Amount and Tenure
The loan amount ranges from Rs 50,000 to Rs 25 lakh. Tenure can be chosen between 12 and 60 months. The maximum EMI cannot exceed 50 percent of the net monthly income of the applicant.

## 3. Interest Rate and Processing Fee
Interest rates start at 10.99 percent per annum on a reducing balance basis and depend on the credit profile of the borrower. A processing fee of up to 2 percent of the loan amount plus GST is charged at disbursal. The processing fee is non-refundable.

## 4. Prepayment and Foreclosure
Partial prepayment is allowed after 6 EMIs have been paid, with a minimum prepayment amount of 3 EMIs. A foreclosure charge of 4 percent of the outstanding principal applies for foreclosure within the first 12 months. The charge is 3 percent between months 13 and 24 and 2 percent after 24 months. No prepayment or foreclosure charge applies to floating rate loans taken by individual borrowers.

## 5. Late Payment and Bounce Charges
A late payment penalty of 2 percent per month on the overdue EMI amount is charged. An EMI bounce charge of Rs 500 plus GST applies for every dishonoured instalment. Repeated defaults are reported to credit bureaus.

## 6. Required Documents
Applicants must submit PAN, Aadhaar or another officially valid KYC document, the last 3 months of salary slips, and the last 6 months of bank statements. Self-employed applicants must also submit the last 2 years of ITR and a business proof.
