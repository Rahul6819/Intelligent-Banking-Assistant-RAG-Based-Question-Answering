# Sample Bank Credit Card Terms and Conditions (SYNTHETIC - for demo only)

## 1. Fees and Charges
The annual fee for the Classic card is Rs 499 plus GST and is waived if annual spends exceed Rs 1.2 lakh. The joining fee is Rs 499. A cash advance fee of 2.5 percent of the amount withdrawn, with a minimum of Rs 500, is charged on every ATM withdrawal. Foreign currency transactions carry a markup of 3.5 percent.

## 2. Interest and Billing
Interest is charged at 3.6 percent per month (43.2 percent per annum) on unpaid balances. The interest-free period is up to 48 days from the date of purchase if the full statement balance is paid by the due date. The minimum amount due is 5 percent of the total outstanding or Rs 200, whichever is higher.

## 3. Late Payment Fee
If the minimum amount due is not paid by the due date, a late payment fee applies. The fee is Rs 100 for outstanding up to Rs 500, Rs 500 for outstanding between Rs 501 and Rs 5,000, and Rs 750 for outstanding above Rs 5,000.

## 4. Reward Points
Customers earn 2 reward points per Rs 100 spent on retail purchases. Points do not accrue on fuel, rent, wallet loads, or cash advances. Reward points expire 24 months after the month in which they were earned. 1 reward point is worth Rs 0.25 on redemption against the statement.

## 5. Unauthorised Transactions and Disputes
Report any unauthorised transaction immediately through the mobile app or by calling customer care. If reported within 3 working days, the customer has zero liability. If reported between 4 and 7 working days, liability is limited as per the Board approved policy. Disputed amounts are credited as a shadow reversal within 10 working days while the investigation continues.

## 6. Card Blocking and Replacement
A lost or stolen card can be blocked instantly from the mobile app. A replacement card is issued within 7 working days. The replacement fee is Rs 200.
