# Sample Bank Savings Account and Grievance Redressal FAQ (SYNTHETIC - for demo only)

## 1. Minimum Balance
The Regular Savings Account requires an average monthly balance of Rs 10,000 in metro and urban branches and Rs 5,000 in semi-urban and rural branches. A shortfall charge of 6 percent of the shortfall amount, subject to a maximum of Rs 600 per month, is levied. Basic Savings Bank Deposit Accounts have no minimum balance requirement.

## 2. Interest on Savings
Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited quarterly. The rate is 3 percent per annum on balances up to Rs 1 lakh and 4 percent per annum on balances above Rs 1 lakh.

## 3. UPI and Transfer Limits
The daily UPI transaction limit is Rs 1 lakh per customer. IMPS is available 24x7 up to Rs 5 lakh per day. NEFT is available 24x7 with no upper limit set by the bank. RTGS is available for transfers of Rs 2 lakh and above.

## 4. KYC Updation
Customers must complete periodic KYC updation every 2 years for high risk, 8 years for medium risk, and 10 years for low risk accounts. KYC can be updated through the mobile app using Aadhaar OTP or by visiting any branch with identity and address proof.

## 5. Account Closure
An account can be closed through a written request at the branch. No closure charge applies if the account is closed after 12 months from opening. Accounts closed within 12 months attract a closure fee of Rs 500.

## 6. Grievance Redressal
Customers should first raise a complaint through customer care, the mobile app or email. The bank acknowledges every complaint within 24 hours and resolves it within 30 days. If the complaint is not resolved within 30 days or the customer is not satisfied with the resolution, the customer can approach the RBI Banking Ombudsman through the Complaint Management System portal.
